What works and what does not
  • Links like youtube.com/watch?v=..., youtu.be/... and YouTube Shorts.
  • Not supported: playlists, live streams, private, members-only, age-restricted, region-locked or DRM videos. This app never signs in.
  • Everything runs on this computer. Only download videos you have the right to save.
